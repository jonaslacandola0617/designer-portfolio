About

Design is how I organize attention.

Profile

Jonas Lacandola is an independent visual designer working out of Pampanga, Philippines — building graphic systems for campaigns, posters, social content, merchandise and brand identities.

His process moves through experimentation, iteration and refinement: a strong concept gets pressure-tested until only the necessary parts remain. He's as comfortable inside a tight brand system as he is inventing one from nothing.
